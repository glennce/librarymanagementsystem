Algorithms and Data Structures: A Modern Approach

Dr. Sarah L. Jenkins

Available in Library

Description

This comprehensive guide provides a modern perspective on the fundamental principles of algorithms and data structures. It covers essential topics including sorting, searching, graph algorithms, and dynamic programming, with a focus on real-world applications and performance analysis. Ideal for graduate students and professional developers seeking to deepen their understanding of computational efficiency.
